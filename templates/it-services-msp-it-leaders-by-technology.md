## Description

### Who it's for
IT service providers, MSPs and software consultancies that sell around a platform their buyers already use.

### What it does
Fill in a short form with a technology, a country and a company size. Argorant counts the IT directors, heads of IT and CIOs at companies that use that technology, saves them as a list and creates a campaign draft with a ready first email. If the count is within your budget, the people are added to the draft.

### How to set up
Set the budget in Settings, open the form, fill it in and review the draft in Argorant before you launch.

### Requirements
n8n-nodes-argorant 0.3.0 or later, an Argorant API key, sending mailboxes in Argorant before launch.

### Costs
Counting, the list and the draft are free. Adding people costs 1 credit per new person with a valid email, only when the count is within max_people.
