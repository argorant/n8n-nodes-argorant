## Description

### Who it's for
Founders, sales teams and agencies who want to go from an idea for a target market to a ready campaign in one chat.

### What it does
You type one sentence such as 'heads of operations at logistics companies in Texas'. Your own AI model turns it into filters, counts and previews the people in Argorant, saves them as a list, creates a campaign draft, writes the emails and adds the people. It checks launch readiness and launches only after you type yes.

### How to set up
Install the Argorant community node, add your Argorant API credential and the credential of your chat model, open the chat and describe who you want to reach.

### Requirements
n8n-nodes-argorant 0.3.0 or later, an Argorant API key, a chat model credential (any chat model node works), sending mailboxes connected in Argorant for the launch.

### Costs
Counts, previews, lists and drafts are free. Adding people costs 1 credit per new person with a valid email. Campaign emails use the monthly campaign emails of your plan.

### Community node
This template uses the Argorant community node `n8n-nodes-argorant` (version 0.3.0). On n8n Cloud and self-hosted n8n: Settings > Community nodes > Install > `n8n-nodes-argorant`. Guide: https://github.com/argorant/n8n-nodes-argorant
