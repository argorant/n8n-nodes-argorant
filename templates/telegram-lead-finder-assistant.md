## Description

### Who it's for
Founders and sellers who want quick prospect answers from their phone.

### What it does
Message your Telegram bot a request such as 'CFOs at logistics companies in Texas'. Your own AI model turns it into filters, counts and previews matching people in Argorant, saves a list when you ask, and answers in the chat.

### How to set up
Create a bot with BotFather, add its token, add your chat model and Argorant credentials and activate.

### Requirements
n8n-nodes-argorant 0.3.0 or later, an Argorant API key, a Telegram bot token, a chat model credential.

### Costs
Free in Argorant: counts, previews and lists use no credits. Your chat model provider bills its own usage.

### Community node
This template uses the Argorant community node `n8n-nodes-argorant` (version 0.3.0). On n8n Cloud and self-hosted n8n: Settings > Community nodes > Install > `n8n-nodes-argorant`. Guide: https://github.com/argorant/n8n-nodes-argorant
